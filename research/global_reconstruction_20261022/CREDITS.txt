This study benefited from tsim through direct use of its closed sampling graph,
signed Pauli-operation reader, commutant measurements, and scheduling as a
research reference. This is direct research use, not a native reimplementation.

  https://github.com/QuEraComputing/tsim/pull/203
  https://github.com/QuEraComputing/tsim/tree/2068ecedee9ef03a8b34bf806c7059fdb42d1196

The reference extraction directly uses zxparam 0.1.0's graph simplification.
Its PyPI source distribution was inspected in the earlier investigation.

  https://pypi.org/project/zxparam/0.1.0/#files
  sdist SHA256: 8cdbfb7fda197b88cfa2b69c5559bb122201974f18d6b355cefd39e5cb824b98

The phase investigation builds on Clifft's existing modulo-eight phase-polynomial
optimizer. Applying the analysis globally on reconstructed affine support was
also informed by the paper previously discussed in this research:

  https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22315v1

Stim supplies an independent stabilizer oracle and Clifford frame construction.
Qiskit supplies independent state-vector evolution for exhaustive toy checks.

  https://github.com/quantumlib/Stim
  https://github.com/Qiskit/qiskit

The preserved benchmark input files were taken from the local pinned benchmark
corpus identified by source paths and SHA256 values in the bridge JSON files.
They are not newly generated substitute protocols. The thirteen toy cases are
study-generated correctness controls and are labeled separately.
